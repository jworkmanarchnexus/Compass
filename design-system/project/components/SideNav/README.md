The side bar holds the logo, product name, the list of tools, and the person's display settings. It is 232px wide (`--side-w`), 60px when collapsed to icons, and becomes a drawer behind a 48px top bar under 860px.

Nav items are 36px; the current page is `.on` (`accent-soft` fill, bold, a 3px Nexus Blue tab on the left) and carries `aria-current="page"`. Sub-pages use `.nav-sub`. Group headings (`.navh`) are condensed uppercase `text-3`.

The logo is never below 96px wide and keeps clear space of about 20% of its width. Use the file in the Brand assets; never redraw it.
