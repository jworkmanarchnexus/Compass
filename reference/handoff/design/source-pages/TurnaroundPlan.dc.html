What the labels mean
ExplanationDana. Why the project is below break-even. The first one opens the plan for the year.
CommitmentDana. A promise with a date. She also picks one lever and an amount.
UpdateDana. What has happened since the last entry.
DoneDana presses Mark done. On or before its date counts as kept.
Date revisedDana presses Change date. Every earlier date stays visible.
MissedThe system. A date passed and nobody pressed Done.
CorrectionDana. Fixes an earlier entry without deleting it.
Admin noteJosh. A question or comment, always labeled.
CurrentOpen commitment and an entry in the last 30 days, nothing past due.
Update dueThe next update comes due within 14 days.
OverdueThe due date passed with no new entry.
No planNo commitment yet this year. An explanation alone does not count.
